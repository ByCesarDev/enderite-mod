🆕 What's New in v1.1.5

🚀 Complete Respawn Anchor Rewrite

  • Entirely reworked system using player dynamic properties instead of entities and ticking areas for more reliable performance.
  • Individual player data — Each player now has their own anchor data stored separately, eliminating conflicts in multiplayer.
  • Automatic spawn restoration — Breaking the Enderite Respawn Anchor now automatically restores your previous spawn point.
  • Improved teleportation reliability — More precise respawn mechanics with better error handling and prevention of duplicate spawns.

⚖️ Enhanced Armor System

  • New custom armor calculation system that correctly applies +4 Armor Toughness and +1 Knockback Resistance to Enderite armor.
  • Visual stat display — All Enderite armor pieces now show their toughness and knockback resistance values in the item lore.
  • Vanilla armor support — System now supports custom stats for vanilla armor variants (Diamond, Iron, Gold, Netherite).
  • Better damage reduction — Improved separation between native Bedrock armor reduction and custom armor math.

⚡ Teleportation Items with Dynamic Lore

  • Automatic charge display — Teleport Swords and Shields now show their current charge amount (0, 16, 32, 48, 64) in the item lore.
  • Ability instructions — Clear usage instructions: "Teleport with sneaking + right click!" for swords and "Teleport attackers with sneaking + right click!" for shields.
  • Real-time updates — Lore automatically updates in inventory and equipment slots while playing.
  • Fallback text support — System includes English fallback text if translations fail to load.

🎨 Improved Crossbow Animations

  • New player animation controllers specifically for the Enderite Crossbow with smoother charge and discharge animations.
  • Better integration with vanilla animation system for more realistic bow handling.

📦 Creative Catalog Organization

  • New "Enderite Shields" category in the creative inventory with all shield variants.
  • Custom icon for the shield category in the creative menu.

 

💡 Pro Tip — Explosive Ore Mining: Enderite Ore cannot be mined directly! You must detonate an explosion using TNT, a Bed, or an End Crystal next to the ore to transform it into Cracked Enderite Ore. It can then be harvested using a Netherite Pickaxe.

 

✨ Core Mechanics & Content

🌌 Void Floating Engine

Enderite and Void items below Y < 1 levitate smoothly with anti-gravity physics instead of being permanently lost in the void. Powered by the stable Script API 2.8.0.

🧰 State-Preserving Shulker

The fireproof and void-floating Enderite Shulker Box preserves item durability, enchantments, custom anvil names, lore, and nested container contents when broken. Now shows its 3D model when dropped on the ground and plays sounds when opened or closed.

✨ Armor Trim Support

Every Enderite armor piece supports vanilla Armor Trims, allowing you to customize your endgame armor using Smithing Templates and vanilla trim materials.

⚡ Teleport Weapons

The Enderite Sword teleports you in your viewing direction using Sneak + Right-Click. The Enderite Shield teleports attackers away using Sneak + Block. Both support up to 64 charges and now display their charge and ability as lore text.

🪖 Enderman Stealth Helmet

Wearing the Enderite Helmet prevents Endermen from becoming aggressive when you look directly at them.

🔥 Fire & Lava Immunity

Enderite and Void equipment cannot be destroyed by fire or lava, protecting your most valuable endgame gear from accidental loss.

🛠️ Progression & Crafting Guide

1. Ore Mining & Smelting

Find Enderite Ore in The End between Y: 12 and Y: 48. Detonate an explosion next to it to transform it into Cracked Enderite Ore. Mine the cracked ore using a Netherite Pickaxe and smelt it in a Blast Furnace to obtain Enderite Scraps.

2. Ingot Forging & Smithing Template

Combine 4 Enderite Scraps + 4 Diamonds in a Crafting Table to forge an Enderite Ingot. Obtain the Enderite Upgrade Smithing Template from End Cities, or duplicate it using 7 Enderite Ingots, 1 End Stone, and 1 Enderite Upgrade Smithing Template.

3. Armor Upgrades — Smithing Table

Upgrade each piece of Netherite armor into Enderite armor at the Smithing Table:

[Enderite Upgrade Template] + [Netherite Armor Piece] + [Enderite Ingot]

Applies to: Helmet · Chestplate · Leggings · Boots

4. Tool & Weapon Upgrades — Smithing Table

Applies to: Sword · Axe · Pickaxe · Shovel · Hoe
